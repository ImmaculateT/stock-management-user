#  Maricopa Stock Management System

**A Smart, Reliable and Efficient Inventory Management Solution**

The **Maricopa Stock Management System** is a web-based inventory management application designed to simplify and improve how Maricopa Concrete manages, monitors, and tracks its stock operations.

The system provides a centralized platform for recording incoming stock, monitoring inventory levels, identifying out-of-stock and low-stock items, and maintaining better visibility of inventory activities.

It is designed to make stock management more organized, transparent, efficient, and accessible through a clean and user-friendly interface.

---

##  Key Features

### 1. Inventory Management
- Register and record incoming stock.
- Maintain organized inventory records.
- Monitor available stock quantities.
- Track stock movements and inventory changes.
- View stock information from a centralized dashboard.

### 2. Stock Monitoring
- Identify items currently in stock.
- Identify out-of-stock products.
- Monitor low-stock inventory.
- Track available quantities.
- Improve inventory visibility and control.

### 3. Administrative Dashboard
- Centralized inventory overview.
- Access important stock information.
- Monitor inventory activities.
- Get a clearer picture of the organization's stock position.

### 4. User Access and Permissions
- Dedicated worker-facing interface.
- Read-only access for regular workers.
- Administrative privileges reserved for authorized administrators.
- Helps prevent unauthorized inventory modifications.

### 5. User-Friendly Interface
- Clean and modern interface.
- Responsive design.
- Easy navigation.
- Organized inventory information.
- Designed to simplify everyday stock management operations.

## 🔐 Security and Access Control

The system incorporates role-based access principles to separate administrative operations from regular worker activities.

- **Administrator:** Responsible for authorized inventory management and administrative operations.
- **Worker:** Can access inventory information through a read-only interface.

This separation helps maintain accountability and reduces the risk of unauthorized changes to stock records.
